One window for every game on your PC.

TurboV finds your installed Steam games on its own, lets you add anything else you want to launch, and keeps track of how long you've played. I built it because I wanted a plain, quick launcher without a store, ads or a login. It's free, and everything it saves stays in a file next to the program.

What it actually does

No big promises, just what's in the program right now.

Finds your Steam games

It reads Steam's own library files, including extra drives, so installed games just show up. Playtime and last-played dates come from your local Steam data.

Add anything else

Point it at an .exe, .sh or .bat, give it a name and optional launch parameters, and it joins the grid. Good for VR tools, emulators, mod managers and random utilities.

Counts your hours

For non-Steam programs it times how long they stay open. It starts them separately, so closing the launcher won't close your game. You can also type in hours by hand.

Looks the way you want

Pick your own colours, background image, font, card size and corner roundness. The Settings button in the demo above does some of this for real.

Notes for each game

There's a little notepad on every game for server IPs, keybinds, mod lists, whatever you'd otherwise forget.

FiveM built in

If FiveM is installed it appears in your library, and a tab lists servers registered with us so you can connect or jump to their Discord.

Little things

  • Double-click a card to launch it.
  • Ctrl+F jumps to search, F5 rescans your libraries.
  • Right-click a game to rename it, set custom cover art, change its exe, set launch options or hide it.
  • Favourite games and filter to just those.
  • Sort by name, most played, recently played or platform.
  • The Random button picks something when you can't decide.
  • Optional Steam API key: more accurate playtime, plus which friends are in a game right now.
  • Checks for new releases and asks before updating. It verifies the download's checksum and lets you skip a release.

What it talks to

TurboV doesn't have accounts or analytics. It does go online for a few things, and I'd rather you know exactly what.

GitHubChecks for a newer release shortly after it starts, then every 30 minutes while it's open. Updates only download if you say yes.
SteamCover art and the store description, genres and DLC for a selected game. If you add your own API key and Steam ID, it also fetches your playtime and your friends' in-game status.
Our server listOnly if FiveM is installed. It downloads the list of registered servers and their logos.

Everything else lives in a turbov_config.json file next to the program: favourites, notes, playtime, theme and your Steam key. The key is stored as plain text there, so don't share that file. Deleting the file resets the launcher.

Questions people ask

Why did you build this?

I got tired of heavy game clients that take ages to load just so I can start something. I wanted one fast, dark window with all my games in it, and room to add the odd non-game program too.

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no store, no ads and no locked features. If you want to chip in anyway, there's a donate page, but it's never required.

Does it only work with Steam?

No. Steam games are detected automatically, but you can add any program yourself, and FiveM is picked up if you have it. Other stores' games can go in as custom entries by pointing to their exe.

Why are some of my playtimes different from Steam's?

Steam games use Steam's own numbers, or the larger of Steam's and anything you've typed in. Custom programs use time the launcher measured itself while they were open.

Where do I report bugs or ask for features?

The Discord server. It's still being worked on, and that's where I'm easiest to reach.
